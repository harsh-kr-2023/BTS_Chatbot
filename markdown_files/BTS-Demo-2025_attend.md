## Why Attend BTS 2025?

Whether you are an entrepreneur, investor, academic, or tech enthusiast, Bengaluru Tech Summit is your gateway to the future. Don’t miss out on this opportunity to be part of a global movement -   

#####  __Unmatched Networking Opportunities

Connect with the brightest minds in tech from around the world—CEOs, industry experts, government leaders, and innovators. Expand your network and explore new collaborations that drive growth and innovation.  

#####  __Cutting-Edge Knowledge Sharing

BTS brings you exclusive access to insights on the latest advancements in AI, life sciences, biotech, climate tech, and more. At the multi-stage Conference, learn from global thought leaders through keynote sessions, panel discussions, and workshops tailored to future-proof your tech venture. Click here to register as Delegate.   

#####  __Experience Innovation Firsthand

Dive into immersive exhibitions showcasing breakthrough technologies. Explore solutions across diverse sectors—from AI and machine learning to health tech and smart cities—demonstrating the transformative impact of technology. Register today.   

#####  __Startup Showcase

With an exclusive focus on startups, BTS 2025 offers a vibrant platform for budding entrepreneurs to interact with investors, mentors, and peers in the exclusive Startup Conference Track. Also discover new ventures, ideas, and potential investments that are shaping tomorrow’s technology landscape at the Startup Exhibition Pavilion.  

#####  __Government and Industry Collaboration

Gain insights into public-private partnerships driving technology-led growth in India and globally. Hear from policymakers on how tech is being leveraged to solve challenges in governance, sustainability, and smart infrastructure.  

#####  __Global Thought Leadership

Join discussions on the future of technology with experts from all corners of the world. BTS hosts a unique combination of speakers, panels, and roundtables, offering unmatched opportunities to engage with global leaders in innovation. Know more about InterlinX- our B2B Partnering tool.   

#####  __Investor access to Innovation

For investors, BTS 2025 is more than just discovering high-potential startups- it is a chance to shape the future of India’s innovation ecosystem. The event provides direct access to startups across diverse sectors and growth stages, a golden opportunity to engage with investors, showcase innovations, and explore funding.  

## Welcoming International Participation at BTS 2025

Whether you're an International startup looking to make an impact or an established enterprise looking to invest in India, find partners or an international trade mission seeking strategic collaborations, BTS 2025 offers various ways to participate:   

#####  __Country Partnership

The BTS Country and Country-in-Focus partnerships offers an exceptional platform for exclusive branding and showcasing their opportunities.   

#####  __Global Convergence

We invite participation from your country as Delegates to connect and collaborate.   

#####  __Country Pavilions

Countries can showcase their technological expertise by bringing their leading tech companies to feature in dedicated Country Pavilions at the exhibition.   

#####  __International Delegations

We invite delegations from government ministries, trade bodies, corporations, top universities, R&D institutions, and industry associations to register for BTS 2025. Join us to attend the event, conduct business meetings, participate in field visits, and more.   

#####  __Collaborations

Numerous MoUs between countries, international state representatives, universities, trade missions, and corporates have been signed with their Indian partners at BTS.   

#####  __Curate Sessions

Under the Global Collaborations Track, Country Partners can curate and conduct conference sessions, roundtable meetings, and educate participants about their strengths and opportunities in their country.   

#####  __B2B & B2G Meetings 

Country delegations can conduct productive meetings with industry and government representatives over the three-day event   

#####  __Global Startup Ecosystem Congregation

BTS 2025 invites startups from around the world to join this premier event, offering unparalleled opportunities for partnerships, collaborations, and growth.
