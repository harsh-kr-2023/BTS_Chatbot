## Future Makers Conclave

In a world racing toward the future, there is one space where innovation meets intention, and disruption meets depth. The Future Makers Conclave at Bengaluru Tech Summit 2025 is a powerful, immersive experience curated for those who are not afraid of scaling the impossible, but with a vision, purpose, and possibility. Set inside BTS 2025, this high-impact, one-day conclave brings together an extraordinary community of trailblazers- from global changemakers and entrepreneurs to spiritual leaders, engineers, adventure sportspersons and athletes, to artists, and pioneers of the impossible.  
  
In an ambitious attempt to make it to the Guinness Book of World Records, 20,000 startup founders from across the globe will come together, to be a part of this gathering, all united by a shared purpose- shaping a conscious, connected future.  

##### What to expect:

Fusion performances | Global keynote voices | Mindful leadership dialogues | Disruptive innovators and game-changers
