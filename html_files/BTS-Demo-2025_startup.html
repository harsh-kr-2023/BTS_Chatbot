Pitching & Mentoring

Pitching
The Pitching Arena at Bengaluru Tech Summit is a high-energy platform where innovation will meet opportunity. Startups at various growth stages get the chance to present their ideas, products and business models to a curated audience of global investors, venture capitalists, industry leaders and ecosystem enablers. For Tech Startups, this will be a gateway to visibility, validation and possibly funding. Whether you are looking to raise capital, enter new markets or find the right partners, the Pitching Arena at BTS2025 will be the place to be. With a strong focus on deeptech, AI, climate tech, biotech, electronics & semiconductors, spacetech and other frontier innovations, the BTS Pitching Arena is where the next generation of game-changing ventures take off.

Mentoring
The Mentoring Sessions at Bengaluru Tech Summit offer startup founders and innovators the unique opportunity to engage directly with a panel of seasoned entrepreneurs, investors, policymakers, and domain experts. These interactive sessions are designed to provide strategic guidance, business model refinement, go-to-market insights and valuable industry connections. The mentoring sessions are highly personalized and will have experts who can guide startups across key tech sectors. For those looking to grow smart and scale right, BTS Mentoring Session is your space to listen, learn and lead.

Reverse Pitching

This session lets investors pitch to startups, sharing their fund philosophy, portfolio focus, and what they are looking to back next. A transparent, founder-first approach to capital connections is what this session will be about.
