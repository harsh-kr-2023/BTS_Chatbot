Conference @ BTS 2025

Igniting Futurise

As we stand at the edge of an era defined by exponential technologies and global urgency, the Conference at Bengaluru Tech Summit 2025 embodies the spirit of FutuRise - a call to rise beyond boundaries, ideate with purpose, and build future-ready societies.

Over 3 power-packed days, the conference will host 8 thematic tracks, 600+ global speakers, 100+ deep-dive sessions, and thought leadership dialogues that spotlight the power of technology across sectors and societies. From AI and semiconductors to healthtech and climate tech to global policy, this is where conversations shape the contours of tomorrow.

Thematic Tracks at BTS 2025

IT & Deeptech

Dive into quantum computing, edge AI, 5G, open-source platforms, and digital public infrastructure with global innovators and tech leaders.

AI Universe

AI is driving real-world value across sectors. Learn about generative AI, foundation models, multimodal systems, and ethical frameworks for responsible AI deployment.

Electro-Semicon

Explore India’s semiconductor mission, including chip design, packaging, foundries, and the rise of Karnataka in the global value chain.

Digi-health & Biotech

From AI-driven diagnostics and health data to agri-biotech and sustainable biomanufacturing, this track advances life sciences and health tech.

Startup Ecosystem

Founder keynotes, investor roundtables, and global showcases—celebrating Bengaluru's role as India’s startup capital.

Defense & Spacetech

Discover how technology is shaping the future of defense and space domains with insights on innovations, partnerships and global trends.

Finverse

Fintech founders and regulators explore digital identity, blockchain, India Stack 2.0, DeFi, financial inclusion, and the future of real-time payments.

My Planet, My Future

Climate innovation, ESG tech, clean energy, and net-zero solutions. Join green startups, policymakers, and environmental leaders.

India-USA Tech Conclave

The 4th edition celebrates bilateral innovation partnerships across AI, semiconductors, cybersecurity, and critical technologies.

Global Collaboration

This track fosters international partnerships through curated GIA sessions, B2Bs, innovation showcases, and multilateral panels.

Session Formats
  Keynote Sessions by global industry pioneers and visionaries
  Fireside Chats that bring out candid insights from top leaders
  Panel Discussions with experts debating the trends, opportunities, and road ahead
  Masterclasses & Special Talks offering deep-dives and skill-building
  Roundtables designed for private, high-impact peer discussions

Who Will You Hear From?
  Global CEOs & CTOs from Global companies
  Deeptech Founders & Unicorn Builders
  Ministers, Secretaries & Policymakers from India and abroad
  Venture Capitalists, LPs, and Family Offices
  Academics & Researchers from leading global Universities, institutions and Labs
  Innovators & Inventors at the frontier of emerging tech
  Startup Founders across various tech sectors
  Thought Leaders & Insights

Why You Should Attend the BTS 2025 Conference

  Witness the convergence of business, tech, and societal impact

  Gain foresight from AI, deeptech, and global digital leaders

  Explore new partnerships with startups, corporations, and countries

  Participate in powerful conversations shaping the decade ahead

  Discover emerging trends across sectors and geographies

  Expand your network with curated sessions and B2B meetings
