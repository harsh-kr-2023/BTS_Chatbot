Green BTS

Building on the strong foundation laid in previous editions, BTS 2025 takes its sustainability commitment to the next level. As Asia’s premier integrated technology summit, we believe that innovation must walk hand in hand with environmental responsibility.

In BTS 2023, we redefined event sustainability by achieving zero waste to landfill, recycling 6,400 kgs of waste, and reducing plastic consumption by 95%. With BTS 2024, we went further—establishing a comprehensive sustainability roadmap with clear goals on circular economy practices, carbon neutrality, and environmental impact monitoring. We also introduced sustainability awards to recognize vendors and exhibitors who champion green practices.

Now, in BTS 2025, hosted for the first time at the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC), we are poised to scale these initiatives. The new venue allows us to integrate sustainability by design—with efficient waste management systems, water-saving infrastructure, and energy-efficient facilities.

Watch this space for more updates on our green journey.
