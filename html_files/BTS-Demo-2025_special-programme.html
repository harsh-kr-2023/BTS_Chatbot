Special Programmes

India-USA Tech Conclave

This focused two-day conclave unites policymakers and tech leaders from India and the USA. Through deep-dive sessions and strategic meetings, it explores bilateral cooperation in emerging technologies, regulatory frameworks, and investment opportunities, strengthening one of the world’s most influential tech partnerships.

R&D Lab to Market

This program features India’s top research institutions showcasing breakthrough innovations. The platform enables direct engagement with startups, investors, and industries to fast-track tech commercialization, driving cutting-edge research from lab benches to real-world impact.

Product Launch 4.0

This focused two-day conclave unites policymakers and tech leaders from India and the USA. Through deep-dive sessions and strategic meetings, it explores bilateral cooperation in emerging technologies, regulatory frameworks, and investment opportunities, strengthening one of the world’s most influential tech partnerships.

STPI IT Export Award

These awards will honor outstanding contributions in IT and ITeS. Winners across diverse categories are selected by an esteemed jury, recognizing excellence in innovation, scale, and global impact

SmartBio Awards

These awards celebrate outstanding leadership and innovation in biotechnology. Selected by a distinguished jury of investors, policymakers, and industry experts, SmartBio awards recognize companies making transformative contributions across biotech sectors in India and internationally. SMARTBIO awards are curated by ABLE.

Startup Ecosystem Impact Awards

These awards recognize Karnataka-based startups delivering disruptive solutions with strong potential for employment, wealth creation, and social impact. Winners are chosen by a jury of investors, policymakers, and business leaders from India’s startup ecosystem.

TCS National Rural IT Quiz

India’s longest-running rural tech quiz returns to BTS 2025 after a grand Silver Jubilee in 2024. Conducted by TCS with the Government of Karnataka, it empowers rural students with IT literacy. The National Finals at BTS will feature top student champions from across the country in an exciting showdown.

  Winner will get Rs.100,000/- Prize
  Runner-up will get Rs.50,000/- Prize

NATIONAL BIO QUIZ

As India's largest national-level Biotechnology Quiz, this programme aims to raise awareness and foster holistic exploration of Biotechnology among students. It showcases country's talent in the fast-growing field of Life Sciences.

  National Winner will get Rs.50,000/- Prize
  1st Runner-up will get Rs.25,000/- Prize
  2nd Runner-up will get Rs.15,000/- Prize
  3rd Runner-up will get Rs.10,000/- Prize

BIO POSTER - WALKWAY OF DISCOVERY

A unique platform for young biotech researchers to display innovative ideas across various areas of biotechnology. Finalists gain exposure, connect with experts, and compete for prestigious recognitions in a vibrant showcase of talent.

  The Top 5 best posters will be awarded with a cash prize of Rs. 20,000/- each
  Poster registration fee is Rs.3,000 plus 18% GST with standard delegate category access
